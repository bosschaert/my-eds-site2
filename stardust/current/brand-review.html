Tensions

Design tensions detected from mechanical analysis of the extraction data.

Single-page depth

The entire product story lives on one long landing page (homepage). The support and privacy pages are lightweight text. There is no dedicated features page, no blog, no comparison content. This concentrates SEO value and discoverability on a single URL.

Cross-promotion section

The support page includes an "Also try RankingsHub" section promoting a separate product. This is unusual for a focused single-product site and may dilute the brand narrative or confuse users seeking breathing-app help.
