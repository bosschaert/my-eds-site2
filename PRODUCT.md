# PRODUCT.md — anthropic.com (Current State)

_Descriptive snapshot of the existing site's brand and product positioning._

## Register

`brand` — corporate/product marketing site for an AI research company.

## Product Purpose

Anthropic builds frontier AI systems (Claude) with a focus on safety, interpretability, and reliability. The website serves as the primary brand presence, product showcase (Claude AI assistant), research publication hub, and enterprise sales channel.

## Users

- **Developers and technical teams** — building with Claude API and platform
- **Enterprise decision-makers** — evaluating AI solutions for their organizations
- **Researchers and academics** — following AI safety research and publications
- **General public** — trying Claude as an AI assistant for everyday tasks

## Brand Personality

- **Thoughtful and measured** — avoids hype; communicates with intellectual rigor
- **Safety-conscious** — positions safety as a differentiator, not a limitation
- **Warm but authoritative** — approachable tone backed by deep technical expertise
- **Mission-driven** — "AI research and products that put safety at the frontier"

## Voice & Tone

Formal-adjacent but not stiff. Academic clarity with startup energy. Avoids jargon for jargon's sake. Headlines are declarative and confident. Body copy explains complex ideas simply. CTAs are direct: "Try Claude", "Contact sales", "Learn more".

## Anti-references

- Flashy, neon-colored tech startups
- Dense enterprise software marketing with feature matrices
- Aggressive growth-hacking language ("Don't miss out!", "Limited time!")
- Overly casual/trendy internet voice

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — minimal visual noise, let content breathe
2. **Warmth through restraint** — the warm palette signals approachability without excess
3. **Typography-led** — serif headlines create editorial authority
4. **Spacious rhythm** — generous whitespace signals confidence and quality
5. **Substance first** — research, safety, and capability speak for themselves
