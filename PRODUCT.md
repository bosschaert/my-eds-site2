# PRODUCT — Breathe (Current State)

> Descriptive snapshot of the existing product at breathe-app.com.

## Register

**brand** — This is a product marketing landing page for a mobile app. The site exists to communicate value, build trust, and drive app store downloads.

## Product Purpose

Breathe is a free, ad-free mobile breathing and relaxation app for iOS and Android. It provides guided breathing sessions with customizable rhythms, calming visual backgrounds (video and photo), and offline-first media — designed to be simple, beautiful, and distraction-free.

## Users

- People seeking simple daily breathing/meditation practice
- Users who value privacy (no accounts, no tracking, no analytics)
- Mobile-first audience on iOS and Android
- International users (available in 27 languages)
- People who want offline-ready wellness tools without subscriptions

## Brand Personality

- **Calm:** Every design choice reinforces tranquility — muted blues, soft shapes, generous space
- **Simple:** "Everything you need, nothing you don't" — the site copy and design both minimize friction
- **Trustworthy:** Privacy-first messaging, free/ad-free positioning, no dark patterns
- **Intentional:** Each feature is presented as a deliberate choice, not feature bloat

## Key Messages

1. Find your calm in minutes (hero proposition)
2. Free, offline-ready, no accounts required (trust signals)
3. Beautiful backgrounds + customizable rhythms (product differentiation)
4. Available in 27 languages (accessibility)
5. Privacy-first — no tracking, no data collection (trust)

## Design Principles

1. **Serenity over stimulation** — The interface should feel like taking a breath, not demanding attention
2. **Clarity over cleverness** — Simple language, obvious CTAs, no cognitive load
3. **Trust through transparency** — Privacy and terms are surface-level navigation items, not buried

## Anti-references

- Aggressive fitness apps with gamification and streaks
- Subscription-gated meditation apps with complex onboarding
- Data-hungry wellness platforms requiring account creation
