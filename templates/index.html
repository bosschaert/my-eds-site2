A glen carved by ice, shaped by saints, walked for centuries

The Monastic City

Founded by St Kevin in the 6th century. Its 30-metre round tower, cathedral ruins and gateway still stand among ancient oaks.

The Two Lakes

Gleann Dá Loch — the "glen of two lakes." The mirror-still Upper Lake and gentler Lower Lake sit beneath sheer glacial cliffs.

The Wicklow Way

Ireland's oldest long-distance trail threads straight through the valley — 131 km of ridgeline, forest and blanket bog from Dublin to Clonegal.

Before you go

Plan your visit

Getting here & parking

An hour south of Dublin via the R755. Paid car parks at the Visitor Centre and Upper Lake; barriers may be down early morning and late evening.

Facilities

Toilets at the Visitor Centre and Upper Lake car park. First aid and a defibrillator at the Brockagh Resource Centre and Laragh petrol station, 1.5 km away.

Come prepared

Weather turns fast in the mountains — bring layers and sturdy footwear. Leave no trace, keep dogs on leads, and don't leave valuables in your car.
