Three things people ask about most

How do the breathing rhythms work?

Each preset sets an inhale and exhale duration, like the 4/6 second Relax rhythm. A pulsing circle on screen shows you exactly when to breathe in and out, so there's nothing to count.

What backgrounds can I use?

Choose from video atmospheres like Beach, River, Fireplace, Earth, and Space, or photo atmospheres like Seashore and Rainforest. Everything downloads once so it works fully offline.

Does Breathe track my data?

No. Breathe has no accounts, no analytics, and no ad SDKs. Your rhythm and background choices stay on your device.
