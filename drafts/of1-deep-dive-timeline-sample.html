The story behind Claude Science

A customizable research workbench built for the tools scientists already use.

Explore Claude Science

Claude Science integrates the tools and packages researchers most often use, purpose-built for scientific research workflows. Here is how a typical research task flows through it, from raw data to a reviewed, reproducible result.

How it works, step by step

  1. 1
    Connect your environment

    Point Claude Science at a laptop, HPC cluster, or Modal account over SSH to manage compute jobs directly.

  2. 2
    Bring your data and packages

    Claude Science works with the notebooks, packages, and pipelines your lab already relies on.

  3. 3
    Run the analysis

    Claude executes code, iterates on results, and tracks the exact environment behind each output.

  4. 4
    Review is built in

    A background reviewer flags incorrect citations or untraceable numbers before you see the result.

  5. 5
    Get a reproducible artifact

    Every output ships with the exact code, environment, and conversation that produced it.
