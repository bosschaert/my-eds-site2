Why it works

Breathing on a slower, longer-exhale rhythm is one of the fastest ways to shift your nervous system from alert to relaxed, and Breathe builds every feature around making that rhythm easy to follow.

01 - The physiology

A longer exhale tells your body it's safe

Slowing your exhale activates the vagus nerve and lowers heart rate. Breathe's presets are tuned around this ratio, so you don't have to count seconds yourself.

02 - The rhythms

Every preset maps to a specific goal

Balance, Relax, Energy, and Sleep each use a different inhale/exhale ratio. Pick a goal and Breathe handles the timing, or build a custom rhythm of your own.
