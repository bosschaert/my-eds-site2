Is Breathe actually right for you?

No app is perfect for everyone. Here's an honest look at where Breathe shines, and where it doesn't try to be something it's not.

Get the app

What works

  • Completely free, no subscription or trial
  • Offline-ready video and photo backgrounds
  • Custom inhale / exhale timings, not just presets
  • Available in 27 languages

What to know

  • No guided voice narration - visuals and rhythm only
  • No sleep-tracking or wearable integration
  • Smaller background library than some paid apps

Bottom line

Best for quiet, quick resets

If you want a calm breathing session without narration, subscriptions, or setup - Breathe is built for exactly that.

Good fit vs. not a fit

  • You want something free that stays free
  • You like ambient visuals over spoken guidance
  • You practice offline or while traveling
  • You specifically want narrated meditation sessions
  • You want sleep or heart-rate tracking built in
  • You need a huge, constantly-updated content library
